# Visual style bible — direction 2

**Approved by the project owner on 2026-11-25.** This is the production direction for the web game and all subsequent Blender scene, character, material, and animation work.

## Direction

Layered paper storybook: a mysterious harbor archive built from cut, folded, and stacked paper. Keep the little traveler readable against oversized furniture and use warm lantern light to guide attention. The scene can feel curious and dusky, while the interface stays legible and welcoming to elementary readers.

## Visual rules

- Palette: muted harbor green, aged cream paper, amber light, muted coral accents, and walnut brown.
- Shapes: broad cut-paper silhouettes, thin stacked edges, slight handmade asymmetry, folded corners, and simple carved/printed motifs.
- Materials: matte paper, card, and painted wood; restrained soft grain; avoid glossy plastic and hard photoreal textures.
- Lighting: warm lantern pools, gentle cool fill, soft contact shadows, visible depth between layers, and low camera angles that make the room feel large.
- Character: original small hooded traveler with simple face, readable cloak, pocket satchel, and warm lantern; no copied character design.
- UI: warm paper panels over a lightly mysterious scene, high contrast for text, large answer hit areas, and clearly visible score/key states.
- Motion: slow ambient drift; paper layers parallax subtly; traveler idle/walk motion stays calm and readable; key unlock gets a small warm light pulse.

## Continuity

Both levels share the same character proportions, paper edge language, palette, and lighting. The market and lighthouse can change props and focal composition, but should read as pages from the same book. All educational labels remain actual HTML text, not text baked into renders.

## Current status

The browser UI uses the selected palette and paper-panel treatment. A Blender 5.3 project, room GLB, 72-frame traveler idle, production render, and Three.js web loader are now in the project. The harbor archive is a shared starting environment; level-specific market and lighthouse props need additional modeling and owner review.
